# CercoZon: Climate Risk Zoning Dashboard for the Peanut Leaf Spot Disease Complex in Major Peanut-Producing States of Brazil and the United States

CercoZon is an interactive R Shiny dashboard for climate risk zoning of the peanut leaf spot complex in the five leading peanut-producing states of Brazil and the United States.

This repository contains the CercoZon dashboard and its underlying geospatial datasets, developed for climate risk zoning of the peanut leaf spot complex in selected peanut-producing regions of Brazil and the United States. The project integrates climatic information, administrative boundaries, and raster-based zoning outputs to support the spatial and temporal assessment of environmental conditions associated with disease risk.

The database includes GeoJSON boundary files and GeoTIFF zoning maps for five Brazilian states and five U.S. states. The CercoZon dashboard allows users to explore current conditions and compare regions, visualize disease risk classes, and obtain spatially explicit information relevant to peanut disease management and agricultural planning.

The peanut leaf spot complex encompasses early and late leaf spot, two major foliar diseases affecting peanut production. By providing standardized maps and an interactive visualization environment, CercoZon supports researchers, extension professionals, producers, policymakers, and other stakeholders in identifying areas and periods potentially favorable to disease development.

The repository was organized to promote transparency, reproducibility, and scientific dissemination. It contains the CercoZon Shiny application source code, geospatial datasets, institutional visual assets, data-processing scripts, metadata, documentation, and instructions for reproducing and using the dashboard.

This project contributes to climate-informed plant disease management by facilitating the interpretation of current and projected changes in the spatial distribution of climate-related risk for the peanut leaf spot complex in Brazil and the United States.
