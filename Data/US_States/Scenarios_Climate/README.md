# U.S. Climate Scenarios

This folder contains the climate risk zoning results used by the **CercoZon** dashboard for five peanut-producing states in the United States.

## Contents

- **`AL/`** – Alabama
- **`FL/`** – Florida
- **`GA/`** – Georgia
- **`NC/`** – North Carolina
- **`TX/`** – Texas

Detailed information about the climate scenarios and zoning files is available within each state folder.
