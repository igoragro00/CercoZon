# Alabama (AL) — Climate zoning of the peanut Cercospora leaf spot complex

Climate favorability zoning for the peanut Cercospora leaf spot complex in Alabama, for the baseline (1981–2010) and future climate normals (2011–2040, 2041–2070, 2071–2100) under SSP1-2.6, SSP3-7.0 and SSP5-8.5.

## Method summary

- **Window:** March–October (AL peanut planting-harvest cycle)
- **Criteria:** mean air temperature over the window + water threshold P* = 58.1 mm/month
- **Agricultural mask:** USDA Cropland Data Layer 2025 (agricultural area = 4.69% of the state; 6,320.66 km²)
