# Minas Gerais (MG) — Climate zoning of the peanut Cercospora leaf spot complex

Climate favorability zoning for the peanut Cercospora leaf spot complex in Minas Gerais, for the baseline (1981–2010) and future climate normals (2011–2040, 2041–2070, 2071–2100) under SSP1-2.6, SSP3-7.0 and SSP5-8.5.

## Method summary

- **Window:** September–April (MG peanut planting-harvest cycle)
- **Criteria:** mean air temperature over the window + water threshold P* = 46.1 mm/month
- **Agricultural mask:** MapBiomas (agricultural area = 60.41% of the state; 356,272.10 km²)

## Raster classes

| Value | Class |
|---|---|
| 3 | Highly favorable |
| 2 | Favorable |
| 1 | Unfavorable |

The rasters cover the whole state (they are not clipped to the agricultural mask); the percentages in the report and in the summary below refer to the agricultural area only.
